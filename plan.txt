# 3. Very important: contribution analysis is NOT anomaly detection

This distinction should be built into your design.

Suppose:

```text
Revenue dropped ₹15M.
```

We calculate:

```text
North      -₹9M   → 60%
South      -₹4M   → 27%
West       -₹1M   → 7%
East       -₹1M   → 7%
```

We are **not saying North is anomalous**.

We are saying:

> North explains 60% of the KPI movement.

That is cheap and useful.

Then we may inspect North's products:

```text
Product A   -₹5M
Product B   -₹3M
Product C   -₹0.5M
...
```

Only now do we decide whether A/B deserve deeper analysis.

The documentation explicitly says contribution tells you **where the change occurred**, not **why it happened**. 

That distinction is critical.
